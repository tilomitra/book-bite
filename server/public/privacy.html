Privacy Policy

BookBite has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Most of what you do stays on your device.

You don't need an account

You can use BookBite as a guest. These things are stored only on your device, not on our servers: your reading progress, finished books, the ideas you keep in your notebook, books you opened recently, your Ask history and your reader settings. Deleting the app deletes them. You can clear your reading progress, recently opened books and Ask history in Settings → Privacy & Data.

If you make an account

We store your email address (and your name, if Sign in with Apple shares it), the books you save and your reading history. We use them only to sign you in and to keep your saved books on all your devices. Accounts are stored with Supabase.

What the app sends to make things work

  • Recommendations. To build the shelves on Discover, the app sends the topics and goals you picked, the books you said you loved and the books you opened recently. We use them for that request only and don't store them.
  • Ask. Your question and up to four earlier turns of the same conversation go to our server and to OpenAI, which writes the answer. We don't store your questions. Don't put private details about other people in a question.
  • Book covers load from Google Books, Apple Books and Open Library. Like any website, they can see your IP address.
  • Rate limits. Our server uses your IP address for a short time to stop abuse.

Buying a book

"Get the full book" opens Amazon or Bookshop.org through our server. We record which book and which store, with no information about you. BookBite earns a commission from qualifying purchases. The store's own privacy policy applies after you leave the app.

Notifications

The daily idea reminder is scheduled on your device. We don't send push notifications and don't know if you turned the reminder on.

Delete your data

In the app, go to Library → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This deletes your account, your saved books and your reading history from our servers immediately. Data that stays on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Children

BookBite is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
